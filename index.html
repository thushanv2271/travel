Top Destinations in Sri Lanka

Discover the wonders of the Paradise Island

Colombo

The lively metropolis of Sri Lanka

The grand city of Colombo is the Commercial Capital of Sri Lanka. Among the high-rise buildings are malls, restaurants and various forms of urban entertainment that awaits the urban explorer.

More Details

Galle

Where the colonial era lives on

Remarkable sunsets and monuments that tell the tales of history in the southern region of Sri Lanka are what the elegant city of Galle is all about. Find out more enticing appeals await you here!

More Details

Anuradhapura

Where the land is soaked in history

As part of the cultural triangle, Anuradhapura is home to a collection of historic attractions that are centuries old where the essence of Buddhism is a prominent part of life.

More Details

Hikkaduwa

The beach lover's sanctuary

Located on the coastal line of the island, Hikkaduwa is a destination that offers sun and beach for the beach lovers. Not to mention the clear waters and coral reefs waiting to be explored.

More Details

Kandy

Explore the last monarch of Ceylon

Being the last kingdom of Sri Lanka, Kandy is treated as a prestigious place to visit. The cultural capital of the island offers iconic attractions and the beauty of nature to indulge in.

More Details

Sigiriya

Where wonder fills the air

Famed the world over for the colossal rock that once served as the residence of a King, the very air of Sigiriya is seasoned with a sense of wonder that is sure to mesmerise its visitors.

More Details

Arugam Bay

Sri Lanka's surf capital

Known locally as "Arugam Kudah", Arugam Bay is one of the most famed and popular seaside retreats found in Sri Lanka with world-class surf breaks.

More Details

Bentota

A charming coastal retreat

Located about 60 kilometres away from Colombo, Bentota is a quaint little coastal town that's full of charm and character.

More Details

Jaffna

Rich in Tamil culture and heritage

Embellished in traditional Hindu flair, Tamil art, and stern yet untainted culture, Jaffna offers a portal for its guests to see an unimposing and fascinating side of Sri Lanka.

More Details

Mirissa

The whale watching capital

Found on the southern coast of Sri Lanka, Mirissa is a seemingly small town found by the coast that is famous for whale watching and beautiful beaches.

More Details

Pasikuda

Azure waters and blissful shores

With shallow and azure waters that extend up to a kilometre towards the ocean, Pasikuda is one of the most blissful beach destinations on the island.

More Details

Polonnaruwa

A World Heritage archaeological city

Being renowned as a World Heritage Site, Polonnaruwa is a city that is riddled with numerous ruins and monuments that speak of a glorious past.

More Details

Tangalle

A charming southern seaside retreat

Located around 200 kilometres away from Colombo, Tangalle is a charming seaside retreat found along the island's southern coast with pristine beaches and a laid-back vibe.

More Details

Trincomalee

The natural harbour of the east

Trincomalee boasts one of the finest natural harbours in the world alongside stunning beaches, historic temples, and warm tropical waters perfect for diving.

More Details

Ella

Cool hills and scenic train rides

Ella is a small town perched in the hills of southern Sri Lanka, known for its green landscape, mist-covered peaks, and the iconic Nine Arch Bridge.

More Details

Nuwara Eliya

Little England in the hills

Dubbed "Little England", Nuwara Eliya is a hill station surrounded by vast tea plantations. Cool climate, beautiful gardens and scenic waterfalls define this destination.

More Details

Negombo

The gateway to Sri Lanka

Located just north of the airport, Negombo is a vibrant coastal city known for its fishing industry, Dutch heritage, and beautiful lagoon beaches.

More Details

Yala

Sri Lanka's premier wildlife park

Yala National Park is home to the world's highest density of leopards, along with elephants, crocodiles, and hundreds of bird species in a stunning wilderness setting.

More Details

FAQ's

Find Your Answers

Yes, absolutely! Sri Lanka offers unique experiences year-round, from pristine beaches to cultural landmarks and wildlife adventures. It's the perfect time to explore the island's natural beauty, rich heritage, and scenic landscapes.

Sri Lanka has plenty of stunning beaches. Hikkaduwa and Mirissa are perfect for relaxing, while Bentota and Unawatuna offer calm waters, golden sands, and peaceful coastal strolls.

Sri Lanka is a year-round destination. The best time is December–April for south and west coasts, and May–September for north and east regions.

Yes, travelers require an ETA for Sri Lanka, except Maldivians and Singaporeans. Apply online at www.eta.gov.lk; it's valid 30 days and extendable up to six months.

There are many ways to travel in Sri Lanka — use PickMe or Uber, taxi services, scenic trains, long-distance buses, and convenient domestic air taxis.

Sri Lanka's currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). Carry some cash for small purchases, though credit and debit cards are widely accepted island-wide.

Sri Lanka's best beaches include Mirissa, Unawatuna, Hiriketiya, Bentota, Arugam Bay, Nilaveli, and Pasikuda — ideal for surfing, swimming, snorkeling, or relaxing, depending on season.

Sri Lanka uses 230V, 50Hz electricity. The main plug types are Type D (three round pins) and Type G (UK-style three rectangular pins). An adapter is recommended.
